# C.5: user groups and group mentions

Routes covered: groups under `/api/workspaces/{slug}/groups`, and `@group-handle` and `@here` in message bodies.
Check: `bash /spec/c/checks/check_c_5.sh`. The rule numbers below match its output.

Do not start this spec until `check_c_4.sh` passes.

All routes are under `/api/...`, return JSON, and take `Authorization: Bearer <token>`. The three HTTP nodes listen on `127.0.0.1:8000`, `:8001` and `:8002`. Status codes unless a rule says otherwise: `200` on read/update success, `201` on create, `400` on validation, `401` on missing or invalid auth, `403` on auth-but-forbidden, `404` on unknown id, `409` on conflict, `413` on upload over limit, `423` on writes to an archived channel. Every request must answer within 5 seconds.

## Uses

Users, workspaces and their owner role, channels and membership, `POST /api/channels/{id}/messages`, the `mentions` list on a message (a list of user ids), `GET /api/channels/{id}/read` (`{read_state:{..., mention_count}}`), and the WebSocket at `/api/ws`.

## Adds

| Function | What it does |
|---|---|
| group store | keeps groups per workspace with their members |
| mention expansion | turns `@group-handle` and `@here` into user ids |

## Rules

`GroupObj = {id, handle, name, member_user_ids}`. Create and update bodies use `user_ids` for the members. Responses use `member_user_ids`, not `members`.

5.1 **Create.** `POST /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups` with `{"handle":"eng","name":"Engineers","user_ids":[a,b]}` from any workspace member returns `201` and `{group}` with `handle`, `name` and `member_user_ids` containing a and b.

5.2 **List.** `GET /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups` returns `200` and `{groups}` including the new group.

5.3 **Not a member.** A user who is not a member of the workspace gets `403` when creating a group.

5.4 **Duplicate handle.** Creating a second group with the same `handle` in the same workspace returns `409`.

5.5 **Update by creator.** `PATCH /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups/{handle}` from the creator with `{"name":"Eng","user_ids":[a]}` returns `200` and the updated `{group}`.

5.6 **Update by the workspace owner.** The workspace owner may update a group created by someone else (`200`).

5.7 **Update by someone else.** Another plain member gets `403` and the group is unchanged.

5.8 **Unknown group.** `PATCH` on a handle that does not exist returns `404`.

5.9 **Delete.** `DELETE /api/workspaces/{slug}/groups/{handle}` from the creator returns `200` with `{"deleted":true}`. The group is gone from the list.

5.10 **Delete is idempotent.** Deleting the same handle again returns `200` with `{"deleted":true}`.

5.11 **Delete by someone else.** Another plain member gets `403` when deleting a group, and the group stays.

5.12 **Group mention.** A message with `@<handle>` has `mentions` containing every group member who also belongs to the channel.

5.13 **Not in the channel.** A group member who is not in the destination channel is not in `mentions`.

5.14 **Author excluded.** If the author is a group member, the author is not in `mentions`.

5.15 **Deduplicated.** A message with `@<handle>` and `@<username>` of a member of that group lists that user once.

5.16 **Mention count.** A group member who is in the channel has `read_state.mention_count` raised by 1 after the message, and the author's own `mention_count` stays `0`.

5.17 **@here online.** `@here` puts into `mentions` the non-author channel members who have an open WebSocket right now.

5.18 **@here offline.** A channel member with no open WebSocket is not in `mentions` for `@here`. The author is never in `mentions`.

5.19 **Self mention.** A user who mentions themselves (`@<own username>`) is not in `mentions` and their own `mention_count` does not rise.
